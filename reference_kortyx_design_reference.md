**Kortyx**, <https://kortyx.co/>, tagline *"The AI that learns how you work"*. He sent it on
Telegram **14 August 2026, 16:20**, and asked about it again on 24 September without remembering
the name.

**His words, verbatim, and they matter more than the link:**
> "This could be a great tool to mimick with just CC in da system. In the way in which it monitors,
> searches well organized context memory/uses context window directly, looks for proactive tasks to
> do, approval request on tg, follows that thread (part of the memory thing, step 1)."

That is the DA SYSTEM brief, written six weeks before the pieces existed: monitoring, an organised
memory rather than a context dump, proactive work found rather than waited for, **approval requests
on Telegram** (which became the ping hub), and threading the follow-up.

**How to apply.** When he questions why a piece of DA SYSTEM exists, this is the origin: he named
the shape himself. And when judging a new piece, the test he set is that list, not novelty.
