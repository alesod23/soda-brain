For follow-ups to Italian practitioners Alessandro already had a real interview/call with (e.g. Paola Castagna at Leonardo), the right register is:

- **Opener:** `Buongiorno [first name],` — NOT `Gentile Dott.ssa [Surname]`. The first call earned tu-form familiarity.
- **Warmth line:** `Spero tutto bene da voi a [Company]!` — anchors that this is a continuation, not a cold reach.
- **Progression marker:** explicitly name the jump — `abbiamo deciso di lanciare la startup e costruito un primo prototipo`. Don't bury it. The reason for the new ask IS the progression from research to product.
- **Register:** stay in **tu** throughout (`avevi sollevato`, `tuo suggerimento`, `quando preferisci`). Don't mix Lei — even one slip ("come da suo suggerimento") breaks the warmth.
- **Mirror their own words:** quote the pain point they gave on the original call back to them (e.g. Paola's "80% interviste inventori" and "no sul non brevettabile"). Proves you listened and built for them.
- **Referral closure:** if they referred you to someone, say what you did with it ("ho scritto a X come da tuo suggerimento; con X ho una call settimana prossima"). Quiet credibility, not bragging.
- **Close:** `Disponibile la prossima settimana, quando preferisci. Grazie 🙌` — no "Cordialmente", no signature on follow-ups. See [[feedback_wa_message_close_style]] for the standing WA close rule (no sig + 🙌 on thanks).

**Why:** Confirmed pattern 2026-05-24 on Paola Castagna (Leonardo) WA outreach. Earlier draft used formal Lei + cold-style opener; user rewrote in tu/warm style and asked to take inspiration from past research→prototype reachouts. The warm tu register is correct for any Italian practitioner who already gave a real interview.

**How to apply:** Default to this style for any Italian-language follow-up WA/email to a practitioner Alessandro has had a real call with. For cold first reaches (no prior call), stay formal — Lei + `Gentile Dott./Dott.ssa/Ing.` (see the Vitillo/Bongiovanni cold-email draft in Notion "Write email to Italian POs"). The transition trigger is: have we already had a real conversation? → tu. If not → Lei.
