Alessandro lives in Munich (CDTM student / Lobbly cofounder) and travels frequently to Italy. Two recurring destinations seen so far:

**Molise / Termoli area:**
- No airport. Closest commercial = **PSR Pescara** (~80 km, 1 h drive / IC train).
- Wider net for cheaper flights: **NAP Napoli** (~250 km, ~3 h IC train), **BRI Bari** (~200 km, ~4 h), **FCO Roma** (~250 km, ~4 h IC), **MIL** / **ROM** hubs if connecting from further north.
- Can usually arrange pickup from PSR ("non ideal" but doable). Family-side hub.

**Sardegna / provincia di Nuoro (e.g. Santa Lucia):**
- Two airports: **OLB Olbia** (preferred — easy pickup) and **CAG Cagliari** (cheaper sometimes, but adds a 3.5 h Trenitalia train Cagliari → Nuoro).
- Ferries from mainland (Civitavecchia, Genoa, Napoli, Livorno) are an overnight option worth considering for cost or for August peak-fare-flight avoidance.

**Why:** Frequent personal travel between home base + family/friend hubs in Italy. Search assumptions should always include flights into wider hubs (MIL/ROM) plus ground onward, not just the closest small airport.

**How to apply:** When trippy is invoked with a Molise / Sardegna destination (or Munich → Italy general), default to the broad multi-airport multi-modal sweep — not the closest-airport heuristic.
