in the future will just be: 'Okay, created a card. It's called this.' A short message as a response, concise".

**Why:** he gives feedback on the phone between other things; a paragraph back costs him attention he does not have.

**How to apply:** file it the same turn (notif_feedback.py for a notification, addrule.py for a card/surface, the
peer for a build) and reply one line: "Okay, filed: <name of the rule/card>." Add a second line only if he must act.
