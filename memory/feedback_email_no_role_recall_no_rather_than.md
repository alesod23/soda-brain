**His words (2026-09-17 00:55, editing the CMIA Emily/Karie/Suinda drafts):**
- *"set as rule never to use 'you are xx' to recall a role, its unnatural"*
- *"never say 'xx rather than yy', too AI i wouldnt use it"*

**Why:** both constructions are tells. "You are listed as the contact for X" tells the reader you scraped a page; "We are a two-person company rather than an established vendor" is a hedge nobody writes by hand. Same family as [[feedback_cite_knowledge_not_the_website]] (naming the source reads as AI).

**How to apply:**
1. Role recall goes INSIDE the ask, as an aside: *"so I wanted to ask you two things, as the contact for CMIA San Francisco"*, never a standalone "You are listed as ...".
2. No "X rather than Y" / "X, not Y" self-description. Say the one thing: *"I wanted to ask to see what opportunities there are for a young, innovative company like us."* A product-description "rather than" he wrote himself (one inventory rather than several partial ones) is his and stays.
3. Drop "we are two people, so I would rather ask than presume/guess" lines entirely; they are the same hedge.
4. Cold emails on a US trip carry the trip line: *"We'll be in California in October to validate the US market."*
