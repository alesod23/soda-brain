Alessandro is a CS student, new to coding (not a software engineering background). He wants everything explained from first principles — what is actually happening, why, not just "run this command". Assume he understands basic CS concepts but not dev workflows, tooling conventions, or "why we do things this way" in practice.

**Explanation style (important):** When doing any task, add 1–2 extra sentences explaining what is happening from a CS/systems perspective so he builds intuition for how things work as systems. Not a lecture — just a little more than the bare answer. Frame it as: what the thing is at a systems level, why it exists, what is actually happening under the hood. Then the command/code.

Avoid SW engineering jargon without unpacking it. Prefer "this is a process the OS schedules" over "just a daemon".
