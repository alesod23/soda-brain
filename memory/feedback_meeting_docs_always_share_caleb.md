When I create a Google Doc that is the agenda, retro doc, or working doc for a meeting
**between Alessandro and Caleb**, I must add `caleb@tundrahealth.ai` to the access list in the
same step that creates the doc. Said as a rule on 2026-09-19 after the 19 Sep retro doc was
created without him.

**Why:** the doc is the meeting surface. If the other participant cannot open it, the doc is
useless exactly when it is needed, and Alessandro finds out in front of Caleb.

**How to apply:**
- Doc attached to a calendar event whose guest list includes Caleb -> share with
  `caleb@tundrahealth.ai` (writer) at creation, before attaching it to the event.
- Same for any doc handed to a named third party in a meeting context: share first, then link.
- Caleb's addresses: `caleb@tundrahealth.ai` (company), `primocaleb@gmail.com` (personal) -
  see [[reference_caleb_email_identity]]. Use the company address for Tundra docs.
- Sharing a doc with a meeting participant is part of creating the doc, not a separate send.
  Publishing to anyone else still follows the normal approval path.
