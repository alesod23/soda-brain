**What he said (2026-09-24), after I listed "hub review page, 43 cards, j/k, commit" under "your work, iterative":** *"here you show me that you don't understand what I intend with 'iterative.' I don't intend me going through the hub review. I mean me catching something on the get-go on one item and being able to tell you about it."*

**Why:** the whole point of the rule loop is that his input is one sentence at the moment he notices ("this sucks because", "this again"). A pass through a backlog is set-up work, the thing he is trying to stop spending time on. Presenting a review pass as the iterative part inverts the design.

**How to apply:** two separate buckets in any plan for him: (1) the loop, where his only act is the on-the-spot sentence; (2) cleanup, a pile he can decide to burn down when he wants, stated as optional and sized. Never mix them, never call a backlog "iterative". See [[feedback_review_page_design_system]], [[feedback_output_goes_into_the_owning_system]].
