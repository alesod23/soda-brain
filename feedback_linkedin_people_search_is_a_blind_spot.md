**Never say "this person does not exist in any public source" when what actually happened is that
WebSearch could not reach LinkedIn's people search.**

On 2026-09-21, at the AIIC Lombardia convegno, he asked three times for the head of clinical
engineering at IRCCS Ospedale San Raffaele. After six WebSearch queries across two passes I told
him the name "non esiste in nessuna fonte pubblica indicizzata" and built the event card around
that gap. He then typed "ingegnere clinico" into LinkedIn search with the company filter set to
Ospedale San Raffaele, logged in, on his phone, and got **five** clinical engineers in one screen,
with seniority markers and mutual-connection counts.

**Why it happens:** LinkedIn people search results are behind the login wall. WebSearch sees
company pages, staff directories and conference PDFs; it does not see the member index. So for
hospital staff whose only public footprint is their own LinkedIn profile — which is most clinical
engineers below director level — my search returns nothing and his returns everything.

**How to apply.** When a person hunt comes up empty, the honest report is *"i miei strumenti non
arrivano alla people search di LinkedIn: provala tu loggato, filtro azienda + ruolo"*, never
*"non c'è"*. Better still, hand him the exact search to run (query string + company filter) so it
costs him ten seconds. And when he does find them, the value I add is **reading the results**:
contractual grade in the headline (*quadro* outranks a bare *ingegnere clinico*), degree of
connection, and mutual-connection count, which is the difference between a reachable warm intro
and a dead 3rd-degree profile.
