1

The shape every loop shares

Six lanes. An event enters on the left, a reader decides what it means for one person, the brain says what is already open, a card asks him, the owning system executes the yes, and his sentence on the card becomes a rule. Teal wires are the paths added on 3 and 4 October: the connection, the reverse pass, the cleaning tab.

2

The machines and the ports

The laptop sleeps, so it owns only what needs Chrome, Windows or him: the CRM writer, the boards, LinkedIn. The box never sleeps, so it owns every listener and every night job: Telegram, the hub, WhatsApp, the brain. The phone runs nothing. Git carries the repos both ways; the Drive folder DA/ carries the two stores too live for git.

The flows, one chain each

Each chain reads left to right: the event, who reads it, where it is written, where he decides, what executes, what is learned. A teal arrow is his gate. A dashed arrow is a path that exists but is off or paused tonight.
