Read this first

  1. Read it. Open a skill's SKILL.md (installed copy, or the mirror in task-land: same file). Start with drafting or hub, the two with the most of your rules.
  2. Say what is wrong. One sentence where you see the thing it made (the "your input" line on each card), or on Telegram. It is filed into the right ledger the same turn; you never edit a skill or a ledger.
  3. See which rule produced a line. Every rule in a compiled skill ends with a tag like [ledger H12]: that is row H12 of its ledger, your sentence, dated and verbatim.

The file links open on your laptop only. On claude.ai they do not open; the Markdown line under each link is the same address for Obsidian or any viewer.

The rule loop

From your sentence to the line a model reads

You say what is wrong once. That sentence is filed the same turn into the ledger it is about, in your words, with the date. A small map says which of your sentences belong together; the compiler turns ledger and map into one screen, with an example in your voice for each rule, and installs it. The code that makes the thing (a draft, a card, a CRM step, a phone notification) reads that screen on every call. Afterwards an observer checks what was made against the same rules and writes one line per check; those counts go back into the next compile, so what still goes wrong stays in front and what never fires steps back.

  1. 1

    You say it

    One sentence, anywhere: Telegram, a card comment, a box on a page, ((rule: ...)) on the daily page, the CRM row.

  2. 2

    addrule.py files it

    The same turn, into the ledger the sentence is ABOUT (you never pick it).

    addrule.py[addrule.py](file:///C:/Users/Alessandro/task-land/_system/drafts/addrule.py)
  3. 3

    The ledger

    Your words, dated, verbatim, never edited: one row H<n> per sentence.

    <SURFACE>-CONTRACT.md
    _system/[_system/](file:///C:/Users/Alessandro/task-land/_system/)
  4. 4

    The skill map

    Which ledger rows merge into which rule, the group, one example in your voice.

    drafts/skill-map-<surface>.json
  5. 5

    compile_skill.py

    Turns ledger + map + hit counts into one screen; checks itself; writes nothing if a check fails.

    compile_skill.py[compile_skill.py](file:///C:/Users/Alessandro/task-land/_system/drafts/compile_skill.py)
  6. 6

    The compiled skill

    Up to 15 rules in front, each tagged [ledger H<n>]; the rest under "Assumed". Three copies: installed, mirrored in task-land, on the box.

    ~/.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md
  7. 7

    Who loads it

    The code that decides, on every call (named in the skill's first line), or a session about to do that kind of work.

  8. 8

    The observer

    Checks each thing made against the rules after the fact; one line per check in rule-hits.jsonl. Never edits, never blocks.

    rule-hits.jsonl[rule-hits.jsonl](file:///C:/Users/Alessandro/task-land/_system/rule-hits.jsonl)

Back to step 5. The hit counts decide what stays in front: a rule that keeps firing comes forward, a rule that has not fired in its last 50 checked cases sinks to "Assumed" (still checked, back in front the moment it fires). A "like" becomes the example of the rule it fits.
