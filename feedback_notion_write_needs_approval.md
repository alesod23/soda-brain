Standing rule set 2026-07-13: I may add details to Notion pages (e.g. Tundra's "Competition" or
"Events" pages) going forward, but ONLY after asking and getting approval for that specific
addition — never write to an existing Notion page unprompted, even when the addition is clearly
relevant and the page clearly exists for it.

**Why:** "we don't want to clutter notion" — Notion is the user's curated thinking/collaboration
surface (see [[reference_notion_mcp_access]], `medtech-brain/_system/TUNDRA-STACK.md`), and
low-friction auto-writes would degrade its signal over time even when individual additions seem
obviously correct.

**How to apply:** Creating a brand-new page (e.g. a new call-log page / a Meetings row) is a
separate, already-established flow and is not what this rule targets — this is specifically about
**adding to/editing existing pages**. Before writing, name the exact page and the exact content to
add, and wait for a yes. Applies to every Tundra Notion write.

in — **never auto-write Competition rows, new Contacts, Raw-Notes moves, or Tasks; always ask.**
Also: the tundra workspace has its OWN Notion-AI agents that own Raw-Notes ingestion + post-call
intelligence + a "System Rules" page — check that page before routing and don't duplicate their
