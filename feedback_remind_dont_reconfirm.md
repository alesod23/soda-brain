2026-08-11, on the Pre-Kickoff speaker mails: *"what made you go with this text? asking for confirmation? a tech check? how come?"* then *"this is more of a reminder, hey just checking in, wanted to remind you, many thanks"*.

**Why:** all three speakers had already accepted the calendar invite (Andi that same morning, Diepold on 27 Jul, Mulle in June). Asking a confirmed person *"does that still work for you?"* a week out **reopens a settled commitment and invites a cancellation you were never going to get**. And offering a "tech check" to two alumni who have done the same slot every semester for years, and who wrote *"wie immer"* / *"es ist wie immer eine Ehre"*, implies they might not manage a Google Meet.

**How to apply:**
- Check whether the thing is already agreed **before** choosing the frame. Calendar acceptances count as agreement; go read them.
- Confirmed -> write a **reminder**: date, time, room or link, how long, who the audience is, what is already set up. Register is warm and light: *"just checking in to remind you..."*, close with *"many thanks, and see you Tuesday"*.
- The only ask should be one they can safely ignore ("if you'd like slides, send them by Monday"). No question that needs an answer, no re-confirmation, no offer of help they did not ask for.
- Not-yet-agreed -> asking is correct. The rule is about register matching state, not about never asking.

**Also the meta-lesson:** the bad framing came from my OWN 3 Aug note in `cdtm-taskforce/progress.json` ("ask whether they still fit and whether they need slides or a tech check"), reused a week later without re-reading it against what had changed since. **A stale note of mine is not a spec.** Re-derive intent from current state before reusing my own earlier plan.
